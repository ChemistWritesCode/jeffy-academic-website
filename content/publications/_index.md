### Current Projects

I am currently working on several research projects:

1. **HIV-1 Resistance Mechanisms**: Investigating the structural and functional changes in HIV-1 envelope glycoproteins that confer resistance to broadly neutralizing antibodies.

2. **Computational Modeling of Antibody-Antigen Interactions**: Using molecular dynamics simulations and machine learning approaches to predict antibody binding and neutralization potency.

3. **Database Development for Viral Mutations**: Extending the HIResist database to include additional viral strains and antibody classes.

If you're interested in learning more about any of these projects or discussing potential collaborations, please [contact me](/contact).
